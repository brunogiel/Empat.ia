# Empat.ia (Design with Empathy and AI), summary

The method uses a main guide, persistent documents, and selectable agents to understand users in depth.

**Core deliverable**: design principles traceable to evidence. Decision rules for product, user communication, and strategy.

There is no single canvas. The work lives in `discovery/`:

- `1-desk-research/brief.md`
- `_engine/state.yaml`
- `_engine/assumptions.md`
- `_engine/decisions.md`
- stage-specific documents

Fixed gate menu:

- Advance
- Deepen
- Question
- Council

Official agents:

- `guide`
- `qualitative-researcher`
- `deep-market-researcher`
- `uxer`
- `sociologist`
- `economist`
- `growth`
- `artist`
- `philosopher`
- `technologist`
- `data-scientist`
- `industry-expert`
- `sme-owner`
- `cab-driver`

`sme-owner` applies when the target includes SMEs, shops, local providers, small teams, or B2B sales to small businesses.
`philosopher` applies when there are invisible assumptions, loose definitions, or ethical risks in the framing.
`cab-driver` applies when lateral thinking, analogies, everyday language, or a non-expert perspective is needed to bring abstractions down to concrete scenes.

The Guide proposes the list of agents for each council with a short justification. The user confirms, adds, or removes before convening.

Source principles:

- Guessing vs discovering.
- Don't start with solutions.
- The interviewee is the expert.
- The guide is a cheat sheet, not a questionnaire.
- User, customer, payer, decision-maker, and operator can be different people.
- Interview mainstream and extremes.
- Experts help, but don't replace users.
- Transcripts and notes come in through `discovery/4-field/_raw/`, are logged in `index.md`, and become atomic evidence in `_engine/evidence.md`.
- No insight without evidence.
- HMW must not carry a hidden solution.
