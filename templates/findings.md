# Findings

Owns: patterns, weak signals, contradictions and evidence gaps across interviews. Does not own: atomic evidence → `_engine/evidence.md`; the design principles → `principles.md`; the final page → `8-report/summary.md`. The analysis guide → `analysis-guide.md`; the coverage of the challenge → `coverage-matrix.md`; one sheet per person → `cases/`.

The distillation between atomic evidence (`_engine/evidence.md`) and the
deliverable (`principles.md`): patterns live here before they become rules.
Update it as interviews come in, not once at the end.

No design principles are written here. The principles live in `principles.md`,
the working notes of the synthesis in `_engine/synthesis-log.md`, and what you
hand to someone else in `8-report/summary.md`.

Central rule: every claim points to an evidence ID from `_engine/evidence.md`.

same combination of axes. With a small sample, write "no profile yet" and stop.
Profiles are named by their traits, not by a person.
