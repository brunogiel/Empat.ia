### 1. Start, rapport and consent

Goal: that they understand the purpose without bias and feel comfortable.

Thanks for taking the time. We're looking at how people [topic]; we're not here to sell you anything. There are no right answers, and you're the expert, because you live this. Is it OK if we record, so we don't miss anything? It will only be used for this project.
*Rewrite the opening and every transition as running text in your own voice: not bullets, not a neutral voice.*

### 2. Context and day-to-day

Goal: routine, role, environment and responsibilities.

- Tell me briefly what you do and what your role is.
- How does [topic] show up in your day? → What happens in a normal week?
- What changes when there is urgency, a peak or a closing?

### 3. Current journey

Goal: reconstruct the real process, step by step.

- Think of the last time [situation] happened. Tell me that story from the start. → And then?
  *Stages of the journey (first contact, choice, use, return) are your map. Never say them out loud; if the person skips one, ask "and before that?". Ask for revisits too: did you go back to any of it later? What for?*
- Who else was involved? What did you use: documents, tools, people, channels?
- Where did it get stuck? How did it end?
  *If this already came up, start from what they said ("in the case you told me...").*

### 4. Concrete experiences

Goal: episodes, not abstract opinions.

- Think of a time it went really well. What happened?
- And one where it went badly. What happened? → What did you do then?
  *Never "was it similar to last time?". Ask "what was different?".*

### 5. Decisions, motivations and incentives

Goal: what drives the behavior. Do not assume the behavior: ask first what place it has.

- What place does [the thing] have when you [situation]? → How does it come into it?
- How do you decide what to do or who to call? What weighs most?
- What would make you change how you solve it? And what would make you not change?

### 6. Workarounds and current alternatives

Goal: informal solutions and real competition.

- What do you use today even if it is not ideal?
- What part have you solved with WhatsApp, Excel, paper or favors?
- Did you try an alternative and drop it? → Which? Why?

### 8. Ideal scenario

Goal: open future needs without inducing a solution. Ask about their ideal month, not the ideal product: the product answer is their design, not their problem.

- If this worked much better, what would have to happen?
- What would a signal be that "this works"?

### 9. Closing

- Is there anything important I did not ask? → What?
- Is there anyone else I should talk to? Can we reach out again if a question comes up?
