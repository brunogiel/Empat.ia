## Sample risks

What can bias who we end up talking to.

- Only friends or acquaintances.
- Only users who are easy to find.
- Mixing very different profiles in a single guide.
- Not interviewing the buyer when user and customer differ.
- Not including extremes.

## WhatsApp message

```text
Hi [Name], how are you?

I'm running some research to better understand how [profile] solve [topic/problem]. It isn't a sales pitch or a demo: I want to learn from your real experience.

It would be a [45-60 min] chat over [video call/in person]. If it works for you, you can pick a time here: [link].

As a thank-you, [incentive if applicable].

Thanks!
```

## Email message

```text
Subject: Research on [topic]

Hi [Name],

I'm running research to understand how [profile] live and solve [situation]. The idea is to learn from real experiences, not to present a solution.

I'd like to invite you to a [45-60 min] interview over [modality]. The information will only be used for this project and we can anonymize your answers.

Scheduling: [link]

[Name]
```

## Scheduling and recording

- Keeper of the material (who stores and processes it): Pending.
- The calls and the scheduling link are created from the keeper's account, with the recording on. A recording lands in the storage of whoever starts or hosts the call; if that is someone else, the file may never reach the project.
- After the first session, check that the recording arrived where the project can read it: Pending.
- A session on a screen (prototype, wireframe, form) is always recorded. Without the video, "this", "here" and the clicks are lost.

## Consent record

One row per person, filled when the consent is signed and before the first session. The signed file is personal data: it lives in `4-field/_raw/` (or `7-validation/_raw/`), never in the report or in `share/`. The four permissions are separate: a person who agrees to be recorded has not agreed to be named, quoted or shown in a clip.

| Person (alias) | Signed file | Record | Quote | Name | Clip | Restrictions | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pending | `_raw/<base>-consent` | yes/no | yes/no | yes/no | yes/no | Pending | Pending |

Before a name or a clip is used in any piece, cross three things: this row, who the audience of the piece is (`participant_names` in `_engine/state.yaml`), and what the contract with the client says about recordings and names. The narrowest one wins.
