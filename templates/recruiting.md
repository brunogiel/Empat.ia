## WhatsApp message

```text
Hi [Name], how are you?

I'm running some research to better understand how [profile] solve [topic/problem]. It isn't a sales pitch or a demo: I want to learn from your real experience.

It would be a [45-60 min] chat over [video call/in person]. If it works for you, you can pick a time here: [link].

As a thank-you, [incentive if applicable].

Thanks!
```

## Email message

```text
Subject: Research on [topic]

Hi [Name],

I'm running research to understand how [profile] live and solve [situation]. The idea is to learn from real experiences, not to present a solution.

I'd like to invite you to a [45-60 min] interview over [modality]. The information will only be used for this project and we can anonymize your answers.

Scheduling: [link]
Incentive: [if applicable]

Thanks,
[Name]
```
