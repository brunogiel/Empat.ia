# Field notes

The drawer for the weeks in the field. Owns: your own emerging ideas while the interviews run, before any of them is evidence. Does not own: what the person said → `INT-00X-<name>.md`; atomic evidence → `_engine/evidence.md`; patterns across people → `5-debrief/findings.md`.

During fieldwork you will have ideas: "it looks like everyone does X", "maybe the real problem is Y". Write them here the moment they come, so they do not leak into an interview note. **Every line here is a researcher reading, not evidence.** It becomes a finding only when people's own words support it.

The `editor` empties this at the gate into the debrief: each reading becomes a hypothesis to test in `_engine/assumptions.md`, or is discarded. It never goes into `_engine/evidence.md` as if someone had said it.
