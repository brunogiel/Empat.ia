# Expert consult

Owns: the short guide for consulting experts, inside the client or from the trade, and the hypotheses that come out of it. Does not own: the empathic interview with the people we study → `3-guide/guide.md`; what is learned in the field → `_engine/evidence.md`; assumptions already open → `_engine/assumptions.md`.

An expert is a **witness**, not a user. They tell you what they see in the people who come to them, and what they think about it. That is useful, and it is a different kind of material from an interview. Ask few questions, directly, and keep what comes back in its own place.

## Questions

Few, direct, in the order of the conversation. A consult is thirty minutes, not an interview.

1. **How they use it in their work.** Pending. *(how the thing we study shows up in their day: when they open it, what for, what they do next)*
2. **What the people they see bring back.** Pending. *(what people tell them, ask them, complain about, get wrong, in their words and with a recent case)*
3. **Their direct opinion.** What do you dislike about it? What would you change? *(the shortcut is allowed here, and every answer is marked as the expert's opinion, never as the user's)*
4. **What a person needs to know, and in what order.** Pending.
5. **Material they can pass us.** Pending. *(anything written they already use or send: a script, a checklist, a message they repeat)*
