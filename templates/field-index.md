## Statuses

- `raw` — the material is in `_raw/`, nothing processed yet.
- `noted` — it has a structured note and minimum metadata.
- `evidence` — its evidence is in `_engine/evidence.md`.
- `ready` — it can be used in the debrief.
- `incomplete` — missing transcript, notes, metadata or consent. Say which.
