# Synthesis log

The working notes of the synthesis. This is audit material: it shows what was
analysed, in what order, and whether the chain from evidence to principle holds.

It lives in `_engine/` because you do not read it — you check it when a
principle is challenged and you need to show where it came from. No line budget:
it is allowed to be long.

## Process followed

1. Review `4-field/0-index.md`.
2. Review `_engine/evidence.md`.
3. Work only with items that have evidence extracted. Mark the gaps.
4. Sort person by person, before comparing anyone.
5. Group evidence into themes.
6. Cross profiles.
7. Separate strong patterns, weak signals and contradictions.
8. Write insights as a new reading, not as a summary.
9. Derive design principles.
10. Formulate How Might We questions.
11. List what is still open.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Every insight has facts or quotes behind it.
- [ ] Quotes keep their source and their moment.
- [ ] Insights cite IDs from `_engine/evidence.md`.
- [ ] Nothing was synthesised from material still sitting in `4-field/_raw/`.
- [ ] There are both patterns and outliers.
- [ ] Contradictions were not erased.
- [ ] Design principles are decision rules, not features.
- [ ] How Might We questions carry no hidden solution.
- [ ] Observations, if any, were processed with the same rigour as interviews.
