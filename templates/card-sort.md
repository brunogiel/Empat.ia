# Card sort

Owns: how a card sort is run and recorded so it triangulates what people said, one person at a time. Does not own: the cards themselves → `3-guide/guide.md`; the patterns → `5-debrief/findings.md`.

A card sort here is a **triangulation**, not a ranking. First learn what matters to the person and why (card to quote), then how they grouped. **Never average positions across people.**

## The three states of a card

- **Placed with a reason:** the person put it down and said why.
- **Placed silently:** it sits in a column, nothing was said.
- **Not placed:** left out, or pushed aside.

Said is not placed: if someone says "column 2 or 3" and the card ends in 4, record both and read what was said.

## Reading across sessions

- Read cards by their reason and by what each person put together, not by the column.
- Keep the interviewer's suggestions apart: a meaning the interviewer gave is not the person's.
- What was missing from the deck is as useful as what was placed; it feeds the next deck and the structure of the product.
- Rankings stay out. If a pattern shows, write it in `findings.md` with the quotes behind it.
