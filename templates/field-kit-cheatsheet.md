# Field Cheat Sheet

One page. Take it to the interview. Blocks, timing, opening question only.

## Blocks

| # | Block | Time | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start, rapport, consent | 5 min | Can we record so we don't miss any details? |
| 2 | Context and day-to-day | 5 min | Briefly tell me what you do and what your role is. |
| 3 | Current journey | 10 min | Thinking about the last time this happened, how did it start? |
| 4 | Concrete experiences | 10 min | Do you remember a time it went really well? What happened? |
| 5 | Decisions and motivations | 10 min | How do you decide what to do or who to call? |
| 6 | Workarounds | 5 min | How did people solve it before? |
| 7 | Ideal scenario | 5 min | If this worked much better, what would have to happen? |
| 8 | Closing | 5 min | Is there anything important I didn't ask? |

## Follow-ups

- Why?
- Tell me more.
- What happened next?
- Can you show me?
- What did you do then?

## Remember

- Leave silences.
- Don't finish their sentences.
- Don't sell or validate a solution.
