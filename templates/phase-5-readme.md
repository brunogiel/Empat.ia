# 5 · Debrief: from what people said to what you decide

This folder holds what the interviews mean once you cross them.

What will appear here, when the field is done:

- `findings.md`: the patterns that repeat across people, each one traceable to something a
  real person said.
- `principles.md`: the design principles. Short decision rules you use later for product,
  communication and strategy.

Nothing goes here that is not backed by evidence from `4-field/`. An empty folder is not a gap:
these files are written once there is something to cross.
