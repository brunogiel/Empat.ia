## Hypothesis, with its refuter

**Idea:** Pending. *(one sentence, in your own words)*

**What would break it.** A reviewer searches the transcripts for these observations on purpose:

- Someone who acted, or chose not to act, because of the very thing the idea says is minor.
- Someone who started from the thing the idea says comes last.
- Someone for whom the thing weighed more than what a person told them.
- Pending.

**Rule:** if 2 or more of these show up, the idea drops a rank. It holds "for some", not "for all", and that "for some" becomes an axis of divergence in `findings.md`.

## Cautions: what does not count, or counts with a caveat

- **Premises the interviewer put in are not evidence.** List the interview, the line and the phrase.
- **Voices with a trade.** Someone who does this work for a living speaks as an expert; keep that apart.
- **Sample.** Name what the sample over-represents and what it lacks.
- **Card sorting is not comparable across people** if the prompt changed between sessions. Read cards by their reason and by what the person put together, not by the column.
- **Transcripts distort names.** A quote with a distorted name is fixed in brackets, never silently.
