# Synthesis

This document turns interview material into **design principles** that are traceable to evidence. The principles are the central deliverable of the method: decision rules you will use later for product, communication with users and strategy.

Insights, patterns, HMWs and top quotes are material that supports the principles; they are not the final output.

Central rule: no insight or principle without evidence.

## Synthesis process

1. Review `interviews/index.md`.
2. Review `_system/evidence-ledger.md`.
3. Only work with interviews that have evidence extracted, or mark gaps.
4. Sort by interviewee.
5. Group evidence by themes.
6. Cross profiles.
7. Separate strong patterns, weak signals and contradictions.
8. Write insights as a new reading, not as a summary.
9. Derive design principles.
10. Formulate HMWs.
11. List open questions.
