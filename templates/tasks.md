# Validation tasks

Owns: the task script for a validation session, nothing else. Does not own: the concept → `6-ideation/concepts.md`; assumptions → `_engine/assumptions.md`; logistics → `2-profiling/recruiting.md`.

The task script for putting a prototype in front of people. **It is never edited
during a session**: you carry a disposable copy,
`7-validation/_prep/VAL-00X-<name>-<surname>-prep.md`, and you mark that one.

Tasks only, and the budget is hard: 100 lines. You hold this page while someone
uses the prototype, so it has to fit in one glance. Everything else has another
home: the concept in `6-ideation/concepts.md`, the questions you are testing in
`_engine/assumptions.md`, the logistics in `2-profiling/recruiting.md`.

You are watching what they **do**, not asking what they think. Give the task, say
nothing about how, and write down where they stop. Ask why only after they act.

**More than one profile?** Copy this file per profile: `tasks-owner.md`,
`tasks-operator.md`. One whole script each, not deltas.

### 1. Start and consent

Goal: that they understand you are testing the prototype, not them.

- Thanks for your time. You cannot do this wrong: if something is confusing, that is the prototype's fault.
- Please think out loud while you use it. Say what you expect before you press anything.
- Can we record the screen and your voice? It is only for this project.

### 2. Context

Goal: remember how they do this today, so you can compare.

- Tell me about the last time you [situation].
- How did you do it? What did you use?

## Words not to say

- The name of the feature you are testing, before they find it.
- "Click here", "go to", "just".
- "Is it clear?" Ask what they would do next instead.
