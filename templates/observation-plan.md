## Possible methods

| Method | What it's good for | When to use it | Risks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contextual observation | See what people do, not just what they say | When the behavior happens in a clear place | Requires permission and time |
| Shadowing | Follow a real process end to end | When there's an operational journey | Can alter behavior |
| Immersion | Live or experience part of the problem | When the team is distant from the user | Don't generalize your own experience |
| Experts | Understand context, jargon, regulation or history | When the sector's map is missing | Experts don't replace users |
| Analogous inspiration | Look at similar problems in other domains | When the team is stuck | Analogies aren't evidence |
| Use materials | See documents, tools, screenshots, objects | When there's a tangible process | Watch privacy |

## Observation

During observations, look at:

- Workarounds and adaptations.
- Objects, papers, spreadsheets, chats, tools.
- Changes in behavior.
- Body language.
- Things that surprise you or seem irrational.
- Things that contradict assumptions.
