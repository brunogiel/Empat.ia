## Rules for this piece

- A count is not a slide: it goes to the notes or the annex. What could not be seen goes to "what is left to measure".
- The deck is built by script from a Markdown source and is never edited by hand.
- A text rewritten by another model comes back as a table of changes (original, proposal, reason), never as a file.
