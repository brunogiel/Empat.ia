# 7 · Validation: put a prototype in front of people

This folder is born when the project runs a second round: sessions on a prototype, to see what
people do, not what they say.

What lives here:

- `tasks.md`: the task script. Hard cap of 100 lines.
- `0-index.md`: the list of sessions.
- `VAL-00X-<name>.md`: one session each, feeding the same evidence ledger as the interviews.
- `_prep/` and `_raw/`: the sheet for each session, and the recordings and consents. **Personal
  data**: check who has access to this folder.
- `findings.md`: what was understood, where people got stuck, and what changes because of it.
