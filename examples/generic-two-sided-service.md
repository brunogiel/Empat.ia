# Generic Example, Two-Sided Service

This fictional example shows how the method can handle a project with two different participant groups.

## Design Challenge

How might we help small operators coordinate recurring service requests with less uncertainty and fewer manual follow-ups?

## Possible Method Documents

- `1-desk-research/market.md`: category context, substitutes, comparable tools and adoption barriers.
- `1-desk-research/knowledge.md`: what the team knows, what it assumes and what needs field evidence.
- `2-profiling/profiles.md`: service buyers and service providers as separate participant groups.
- `3-guide/guide.md`: one guide for buyers and one guide for providers.
- `_engine/evidence.md`: quotes, facts, workarounds and contradictions by interview ID.
- `5-debrief/principles.md`: patterns, insights, design principles and HMW questions.

## Note

The example is fictional. It exists to test the method structure without exposing private project material.
